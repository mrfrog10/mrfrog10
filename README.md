## Featured projects

### [Mobile payments: user behaviour and fraud analysis](https://github.com/mrfrog10/paysim-mobile-payments-databricks)
*Databricks · SQL · PySpark · Delta · AI/BI dashboard*

6.36 million mobile-money transactions, analysed for usage, retention and fraud.
- Found that the built-in fraud flag caught only 0.2% of fraud, while a simple "account drained" rule caught 97.6% with no false alarms.
- Showed that fraud is a night-time problem: about 1 in 5 transactions between 02:00 and 04:59 is fraud.
- Ran 12 data-quality checks and verified the key numbers separately in DuckDB.

### [Visual analytics for PostNord Logistics](https://github.com/mrfrog10/postnord-visual-analytics)
*Tableau Prep · Tableau Desktop · Python*

Tableau Prep flows and six dashboards built on 3.2 million logistics shipments and lecture-video engagement data.
- Merged 100 monthly files and removed 1,504 duplicated rows. Aggregated 3.2M rows to 930K with every total unchanged.
- Found that three segments bring 95% of revenue, and that growth comes from existing customers even as the number of active customers falls.
- Built dashboards for sales and product managers with click-to-filter actions and a colour-blind-safe palette.
